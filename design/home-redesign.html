Questions families ask first

Short answers to what most people want to know before they call.

What does it cost to hire you?

Nothing up front. We work on contingency: our fee comes out of what we recover for you. If we don’t win, you owe us no fee.

How long do I have to file a claim?

In California, most personal injury and wrongful death claims must be filed within two years. Claims against a city, county or state agency usually need a written claim within six months. Medical malpractice has its own shorter rules. Call us early so no deadline is missed.

Who can bring a wrongful death claim?

Usually a spouse or registered domestic partner and the children of the person who died. In some families, parents, stepchildren or others who depended on them can also file. We’ll tell you where your family stands.

Do I have to come to an office?

No. We make home and hospital visits, and much of the work can happen by phone. Just ask when you call.
